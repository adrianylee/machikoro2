# Card illustrations

Each of the 40 base-game card types has an original miniature-city illustration. The artwork was generated from original subject descriptions in a hand-painted wood and paper clay style. Four groups were generated as image atlases and exported as individual square WebP files. Bakery, Wheat Field, Radio Tower, and TV Station were generated as individual illustrations.

The application serves these files from `public/art`. Each filename matches its card identifier. The publisher's printed cards were consulted for gameplay data.

The table bank is an original transparent illustration generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and served from `public/table-bank.webp`.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: transparent sprite for the Machi Koro 2 digital tabletop. One original miniature neighborhood bank, three-quarter isometric view. Warm cream limestone classical façade, small jade-blue copper roof, tall windows, broad entry steps, one discreet round gold coin emblem above the door. Detailed hand-painted toy-city diorama, soft highlights and contact shadows, warm material colors, like an isometric collectible board game building. Center the entire object, including the edges of its foundation, within a square composition. Truly transparent background. No text, no card frame, no numbers, no interface.
